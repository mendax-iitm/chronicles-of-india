# Courtesy Note to the Author of `jiangshan-map`

Dear Dean Chen (`@DeanChensj`),

We wanted to share a brief note of appreciation for your work on [**Chronicles of the Realm (`jiangshan-map`)**](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map).

Your atlas demonstrated how a zero-dependency, browser-based historical map with a scrubbable timeline, ghost borders, place name genealogies, person chronicles, and pinpoint quizzes can make centuries of history immediately tangible for students and teachers.

Inspired by your product vision, we built **Chronicles of India** ([https://mendax-iitm.github.io/chronicles-of-india/](https://mendax-iitm.github.io/chronicles-of-india/)) for the Indian subcontinent (600 BCE – 1947 CE). All code, region geometry, and historical datasets in Chronicles of India were written completely from scratch (MIT-licensed code and CC BY 4.0 data), reusing only the conceptual ideas and educational spirit of your project.

Thank you for setting such a high bar for open-source educational cartography.

Warm regards,  
The Chronicles of India Contributors
