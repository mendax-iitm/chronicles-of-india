The Mahajanapadas

महाजनपद
c. 550 BCE · Span: 600 BCE to 346 BCE
Sixteen great realms (mahajanapadas) arise across the Indo-Gangetic plain as iron tools, rice farming and walled cities spread. Magadha, Kosala, Avanti and Vatsa compete for supremacy while Buddhism and Jainism emerge.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Gandhara: Capital Takshashila, famed seat of Vedic and classical learning.
  • Kamboja & Indus tribes: Northwestern frontier peoples; part of the Achaemenid empire from c. 518 BCE.
  • Kuru–Panchala: Vedic heartland with capitals at Hastinapura, Ahichchhatra, and Kampilya.
  • Matsya & Surasena: Matsya capital Viratnagar; Surasena capital Mathura on the Yamuna.
  • Avanti: Capital Ujjayini (Ujjain); ruled by King Pradyota, major center on southern routes.
  • Kosala: Capital Shravasti; ruled by King Prasenajit, rival to Magadha.
  • Vatsa: Capital Kaushambi on the Yamuna; ruled by King Udayana.
  • Magadha: Haryanka and Shishunaga dynasties; capital at Rajagriha.
  • Kalinga: Maritime kingdom on the Bay of Bengal coast.
  • Deccan chiefdoms: Asmaka/Assaka and early iron-using pastoralist communities.
  • Tamilakam chiefdoms: Early lineage polities of the Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas.
  • Island kingdoms (Lanka): Early Indo-Aryan and indigenous settlements; King Vijaya's traditional arrival.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Bimbisara (Haryanka) · 544 BCE–492 BCE
  • Ajatashatru (Haryanka) · 492 BCE–460 BCE
  • Shishunaga (Shishunaga) · 413 BCE–395 BCE
  • Kalashoka (Shishunaga) · 395 BCE–367 BCE
