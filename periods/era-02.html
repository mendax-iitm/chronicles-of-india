The Nanda Empire

नंद साम्राज्य
c. 330 BCE · Span: 345 BCE to 322 BCE
Mahapadma Nanda unites northern India into the first pan-Gangetic empire, fielding enormous standing armies. In 326 BCE Alexander of Macedon invades the Punjab but halts at the Beas river.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Nanda Empire: Vast imperial army reported by Greek sources: 200,000 infantry, 80,000 horse, 6,000 war elephants.
  • Gandhara & Punjab kingdoms: Kingdom of Porus (Pauravas) and Taxila, confronting Alexander's campaign.
  • Indus & frontier republics: Malloi, Oxydrakai and other autonomous tribal republics.
  • Rajasthan–Gujarat tribes: Pastoral and mercantile clans in western India.
  • Deccan chiefdoms: Megalithic cultures and emerging Satavahana ancestors.
  • Tamilakam: Chera, Chola, and Pandya realms trading with the Mediterranean.
  • Lanka: Kingdom of Anuradhapura under Pandukabhaya.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Mahapadma Nanda (Nanda) · 345 BCE–329 BCE
  • Dhana Nanda (Nanda) · 329 BCE–321 BCE
