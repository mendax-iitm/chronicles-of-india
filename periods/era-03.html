Rise of the Mauryas

मौर्यों का उदय
c. 300 BCE · Span: 321 BCE to 262 BCE
Chandragupta Maurya and Chanakya overthrow the Nandas, defeat Seleucus I Nicator, and extend imperial rule to the Hindu Kush. Bindusara pushes southward into Karnataka.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Maurya Empire: Chandragupta and Bindusara rule from Pataliputra; Seleucid alliance sealed.
  • Kalinga: Independent mercantile and naval republic fiercely resisting Maurya hegemony.
  • Deccan chiefdoms: Tribal and pastoral polities entering tributary relations with Pataliputra.
  • Tamilakam: Southern kingdoms maintaining independent friendly relations (Asampradana).
  • Lanka: Anuradhapura kingdom under Mutasiva.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Dhana Nanda (Nanda) · 329 BCE–321 BCE
  • Chandragupta Maurya (Maurya) · 321 BCE–297 BCE
  • Bindusara Amitraghata (Maurya) · 297 BCE–273 BCE
  • Ashoka the Great (Maurya) · 268 BCE–232 BCE
