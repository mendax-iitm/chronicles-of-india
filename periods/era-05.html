Shungas & Indo-Greeks

शुंग और भारत-यूनानी
c. 150 BCE · Span: 185 BCE to 101 BCE
Pushyamitra Shunga assassinates Brihadratha, establishing the Shunga dynasty at Pataliputra and Vidisha. Demetrius and Menander I expand Indo-Greek rule into Punjab and the Indus valley.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Shunga Empire: Pushyamitra and Agnimitra rule from Pataliputra and Vidisha; revival of Vedic sacrifices.
  • Indo-Greek Kingdom: Menander I (King Milinda) rules from Sagala (Sialkot); patron of Buddhism.
  • Satavahana (early): Simuka and Satakarni I establish Deccan power centered at Pratishthana.
  • Kalinga (Mahameghavahana): Revived independence after the collapse of the Mauryas.
  • Western Tribes & Republics: Autonomous clans controlling trans-Thar and coastal trade routes.
  • Tamil Kingdoms: Early Sangam flourishing: trade with Ptolemaic Egypt and Rome.
  • Lanka: Dutthagamani unifies the island kingdom under Anuradhapura.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Brihadratha (Maurya) · 187 BCE–180 BCE
  • Pushyamitra Shunga (Shunga) · 185 BCE–149 BCE
  • Menander I Soter (Milinda) (Indo-Greek) · 165 BCE–130 BCE
  • Kharavela (Mahameghavahana (Chedi)) · 193 BCE–155 BCE
