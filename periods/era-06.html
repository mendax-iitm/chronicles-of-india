Kanvas & Indo-Scythians

कण्व और शक (इंडो-सीथियन)
c. 70 BCE · Span: 100 BCE to 31 BCE
Sakas (Indo-Scythians) push through the Bolan pass into Gandhara and Gujarat. The Kanva dynasty succeeds the Shungas in Magadha, while the Satavahanas consolidate the northern Deccan.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Indo-Scythian (Saka) realms: Maues and Azes I establish Sakasthana across the Indus basin and coastal Gujarat.
  • Kanva & Shunga remnants: Vasudeva Kanva overthrows Devabhuti in 73 BCE; reduced Gangetic realm.
  • Satavahana Empire: Expanding north into Malwa; patronizing rock-cut chaityas at Karle and Nashik.
  • Kalinga: Mahameghavahana power consolidates ahead of Kharavela's accession.
  • Tribal Republics (Yaudheya, Malava): Warrior republics issuing coins inscribed with 'Yaudheyanaam Jayah'.
  • Sangam Kingdoms: Pandya, Chola, and Chera ports bustling with Roman gold coin trade.
  • Lanka: King Vattagamani Abhaya commits the Theravada Pali Canon to writing at Aluvihara.

Key Rulers in this Era
