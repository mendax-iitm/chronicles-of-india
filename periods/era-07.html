Kharavela & Western Kshatrapas

खारवेल और पश्चिमी क्षत्रप
c. 1 BCE · Span: 30 BCE to 29 CE
Emperor Kharavela of Kalinga launches extensive military campaigns across northern and southern India. Indo-Parthians and Western Kshatrapas govern the northwest and Gujarat.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Kalinga (Kharavela): Kharavela records victories over Magadha, the Satavahanas, and the Tamil confederacy in the Hathigumpha inscription.
  • Northern & Western Kshatrapas: Hagamasha and Rajuvula in Mathura; Bhumaka founding the Kshaharata dynasty in Gujarat.
  • Satavahanas: Navigating dynastic challenges while maintaining hold of the western Deccan ports.
  • Magadha & Eastern Chiefdoms: Post-Kanva local rulers centered at Pataliputra and Kausambi.
  • Sangam Kingdoms: Flourishing era of early Tamil epic literature (Silappatikaram era).
  • Lanka: Anuradhapura monastic expansions under the Lambakanna dynasty.
