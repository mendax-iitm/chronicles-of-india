Kushan Empire & Kshatrapas

कुषाण साम्राज्य और क्षत्रप
c. 100 CE · Span: 30 CE to 149 CE
Kujula Kadphises and Kanishka I build a massive trans-continental empire connecting the Gangetic basin to the Silk Road. The Western Kshatrapas dominate Gujarat and Malwa.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Kushan Empire: Twin imperial capitals at Purushapura (Peshawar) and Mathura; Fourth Buddhist Council.
  • Western Kshatrapas: Nahapana and Chashtana rule western ports; Rudradaman repairs Sudarshana lake.
  • Satavahana Empire: Gautamiputra Satakarni destroys the Kshaharata clan, restoring Deccan dominance.
  • Kalinga & Eastern Kingdoms: Independent coastal principalities with vibrant maritime trade with Southeast Asia.
  • Sangam Kingdoms: Karikala Chola builds the Grand Anicut dam; Madurai capital of the Pandyas.
  • Lanka: King Gajabahu I establishes friendly diplomatic ties with the Cheras.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Gautamiputra Satakarni (Satavahana) · 106 CE–130 CE
  • Rudradaman I (Western Kshatrapas (Kardamaka)) · 130 CE–150 CE
  • Kanishka I the Great (Kushan) · 127 CE–150 CE
