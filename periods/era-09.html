Satavahana Zenith & Later Kushans

सातवाहन उत्कर्ष और उत्तरवर्ती कुषाण
c. 190 CE · Span: 150 CE to 249 CE
Vashishtiputra Pulumavi and Yajna Sri Satakarni rule from coast to coast with maritime ship-motif coins. The Kushans stabilize the northwest under Huvishka and Vasudeva I.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Satavahana Empire: Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda stupas achieve magnificent marble-carved artistic peaks.
  • Later Kushans: Vasudeva I adopts Shaivism as power retreats to the northwest against Sassanid pressure.
  • Western Kshatrapas (Kardamaka): Kardamaka dynasty maintaining mints at Ujjain and coastal ports.
  • Magadha & Murunda chiefs: Licchavis and Murundas assert independence along the middle Ganga.
  • Tamilakam: Late Sangam era; emergence of early Kalabhra migration waves.
  • Lanka: King Voharika Tissa reforms judicial law and patronizes Mahavihara.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Rudradaman I (Western Kshatrapas (Kardamaka)) · 130 CE–150 CE
  • Kanishka I the Great (Kushan) · 127 CE–150 CE
