The Pre-Gupta Multipolar Age

गुप्त-पूर्व बहुध्रुवीय काल
c. 300 CE · Span: 250 CE to 319 CE
The Satavahanas disintegrate into the Vakatakas, Ikshvakus, and Pallavas. The Nagas rule Mathura and Padmavati, while the Licchavis rise in Magadha, setting the stage for the Gupta rise.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Vakataka Kingdom: Vindhyashakti and Pravarasena I perform imperial ashvamedha sacrifices.
  • Nagas of Mathura & Padmavati: Bharashiva Nagas performing ten horse sacrifices (Dashashwamedha) at Varanasi.
  • Licchavis & Early Guptas: Sri Gupta and Ghatotkacha lay foundations; matrimonial alliance with Licchavis.
  • Ikshvaku & Salankayana: Successors to the Satavahanas in coastal Andhra at Vijayapuri.
  • Pallavas of Kanchi: Simhavarman establishes early Pallava rule over northern Tamil plain.
  • Western Kshatrapas: Continuing rule in Kathiawar before eventual Gupta conquest.
  • Indo-Sassanids & Kidarites: Sassanid Kushanshahs controlling the northwest marches.
  • Lanka: King Mahasena builds the colossal Jetavanaramaya stupa, tallest brick structure in the world.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Chandragupta I (Gupta) · 319 CE–335 CE
