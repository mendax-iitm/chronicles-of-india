The Imperial Guptas

गुप्त साम्राज्य का स्वर्ण युग
c. 400 CE · Span: 320 CE to 449 CE
Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, and Chandragupta II Vikramaditya unite northern India in a classical golden age of Sanskrit literature, mathematics, astronomy, and rock-cut temple art.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Gupta Empire: Directly administered heartland with western maritime ports annexed under Vikramaditya.
  • Vakataka Empire: Allied matrimonial dynasty (Prabhavatigupta); creators of the Ajanta cave masterpieces.
  • Pallavas of Kanchi: Vishnugopa and successors acknowledge Gupta sphere; building stone temples.
  • Kadambas & Western Gangas: Mayurasharma founds Kadamba power at Banavasi, earliest indigenous Kannada rulers.
  • Kidarites & Northwest States: Kidarite Hunas asserting control in Bactria and Gandhara.
  • Kamarupa (Varman Dynasty): Pushyavarman establishes Kamarupa dynasty in the Brahmaputra valley as a Gupta tributary.
  • Lanka: King Dhatusena and Kashyapa build the magnificent Sigiriya rock fortress.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Chandragupta I (Gupta) · 319 CE–335 CE
  • Samudragupta (Gupta) · 335 CE–375 CE
  • Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (Gupta) · 375 CE–415 CE
  • Kumaragupta I (Gupta) · 415 CE–455 CE
