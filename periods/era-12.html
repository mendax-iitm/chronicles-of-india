Huna Invasions & Gupta Decline

हूण आक्रमण और गुप्त पतन
c. 510 CE · Span: 450 CE to 549 CE
Alchon Hunas under Toramana and Mihirakula ravage the northwest and Malwa. Skandagupta repulses initial waves, but imperial finance collapses. Yashodharman of Malwa defeats Mihirakula in 528 CE.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Alchon Huna Empire: Toramana and Mihirakula rule from Sakala (Sialkot); aggressive westward campaigns.
  • Gupta Remnants & Yashodharman: Yashodharman erects victory pillars at Sondani (Mandsaur) defeating the Hunas.
  • Maitrakas of Vallabhi: Bhatarka establishes independent Maitraka dynasty; Vallabhi becomes a great Buddhist university.
  • Vakatakas & Early Chalukyas: Harishena's Vakataka zenith followed by collapse; Chalukya Pulakeshin I fortifies Badami (543).
  • Kadambas & Gangas: Prosperous rule across the Kannada plateau.
  • Pallavas of Kanchi: Simhavarman II maintains stronghold over Tondaimandalam.
  • Lanka: Moggallana I returns rule to Anuradhapura.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Kumaragupta I (Gupta) · 415 CE–455 CE
  • Skandagupta (Gupta) · 455 CE–467 CE
