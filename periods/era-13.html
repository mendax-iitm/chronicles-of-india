Rise of Kannauj & Badami

कन्नौज और बादामी का उदय
c. 580 CE · Span: 550 CE to 605 CE
The Maukharis make Kannauj the supreme city of north India. The Pushyabhutis rise at Thanesar, Shashanka establishes an independent Gauda in Bengal, and the Chalukyas dominate the Deccan from Badami.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Maukhari Kingdom (Kannauj): Ishanavarman assumes Maharajadhiraja, checking Huna and Gauda forces.
  • Pushyabhuti (Thanesar): Prabhakaravardhana builds martial strength in eastern Punjab; marries daughter to Maukhari king.
  • Gauda Kingdom (Shashanka): Shashanka becomes first unified sovereign king of Bengal, warring with Kannauj.
  • Chalukyas of Badami: Kirtivarman I and Mangalesha expand across the western coast and Konkan.
  • Pallavas of Kanchi: Simhavishnu overthrows the Kalabhras, establishing the Imperial Pallavas.
  • Maitrakas of Vallabhi: Dharasena II and Shiladitya I rule prosperous maritime Gujarat.
  • Kashmir & Frontier States: Rai dynasty of Sindh and early Karkota foundations in Kashmir.
  • Lanka: King Aggabodhi I patronizes Sinhala literature and tank engineering.
