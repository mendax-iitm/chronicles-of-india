Harsha & Pulakeshin II

हर्षवर्धन और पुलकेशिन् द्वितीय
c. 630 CE · Span: 606 CE to 647 CE
Harshavardhana unites northern India from Kannauj. In the Deccan, Chalukya Pulakeshin II halts Harsha on the Narmada, while Narasimhavarman I Pallava takes Vatapi. Xuanzang records this vibrant era.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Harsha’s Empire: Harsha rules from Kannauj; recorded by court poet Bana and pilgrim Xuanzang.
  • Chalukyas of Badami: Pulakeshin II defeats Harsha at the Narmada and appoints brother Kubja Vishnuvardhana at Vengi.
  • Pallavas of Kanchipuram: Narasimhavarman I 'Mamalla' defeats Pulakeshin II and builds the monolithic rathas of Mahabalipuram.
  • Kamarupa (Bhaskaravarman): Allied with Harsha against Gauda; Xuanzang visits his court in Pragjyotishpura.
  • Maitrakas of Vallabhi: Harsha's daughter marries Dhruvasena II, creating dynastic peace.
  • Rai Dynasty (Sindh): Buddhist-Hindu dynasty ruling the Indus lower valley from Alor.
  • Karkota Dynasty (Kashmir): Durlabhavardhana founds the Karkota dynasty, securing the northern trade corridors.
  • Lanka: Prince Manavamma flees to Kanchipuram, enlisting Pallava military support.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Pulakeshin II (Chalukyas of Badami) · 609 CE–642 CE
  • Harshavardhana (Pushyabhuti (Vardhana)) · 606 CE–647 CE
  • Narasimhavarman I Mamalla (Pallava) · 630 CE–668 CE
