Post-Harsha Rivals & Arab Conquest of Sindh

हर्षोत्तर काल और सिंध विजय
c. 720 CE · Span: 648 CE to 749 CE
Lalitaditya of Kashmir and Yashovarman of Kannauj campaign across north India. In 712 CE, Muhammad bin Qasim establishes Umayyad Arab rule in Sindh, while the Chalukyas and Pallavas war in the South.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Karkota Empire (Kashmir): Lalitaditya Muktapida builds the Martand Sun Temple and campaigns across central and northern India.
  • Kingdom of Kannauj (Yashovarman): Yashovarman revives Kannauj; court poet Vakpati composes the Prakrit epic Gaudavaho.
  • Arab Caliphate Province of Sindh: Muhammad bin Qasim defeats Raja Dahir in 712 CE, establishing rule from Mansura and Multan.
  • Chalukyas of Badami: Vikramaditya II invades Kanchi, leaving inscriptions at Kailasanatha temple without plundering it.
  • Pallavas of Kanchi: Narasimhavarman II Rajasimha builds the Shore Temple at Mahabalipuram and Kailasanatha temple.
  • Pandyas of Madurai: Kochadaiyan Ranadhiran expands First Pandya Empire against Pallavas and Cheras.
  • Bengal under Matsyanyaya: Internal chaos and external raids preceding the election of Gopala in 750 CE.
  • Lanka: Manavamma establishes the Second Lambakanna dynasty at Anuradhapura.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Narasimhavarman I Mamalla (Pallava) · 630 CE–668 CE
  • Lalitaditya Muktapida (Karkota of Kashmir) · 724 CE–760 CE
