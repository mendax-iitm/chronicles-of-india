The Tripartite Struggle Begins

त्रिपक्षीय संघर्ष का आरंभ
c. 800 CE · Span: 750 CE to 849 CE
Dantidurga overthrows the Chalukyas, founding the Rashtrakuta Empire at Ellora. The Palas under Dharmapala and Gurjara-Pratiharas under Vatsaraja clash for imperial control of Kannauj.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Rashtrakuta Empire: Govinda III campaigns to the Himalayas; Krishna I cuts the monolithic Kailasa temple at Ellora.
  • Pala Empire: Dharmapala crowns Chakrayudha at Kannauj; patronizes Nalanda and founds Vikramashila.
  • Gurjara-Pratihara: Vatsaraja and Nagabhata II repulse Arab raids and push east toward the Gangetic doab.
  • Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi: Autonomous cadet branch reigning in coastal Andhra.
  • Pallavas & Pandyas: Dantivarman Pallava and Srimara Srivallabha Pandya fight for dominance in the Kaveri basin.
  • Sindh (Emirates of Mansura & Multan): Habbari dynasty establishes hereditary autonomous emirate at Mansura.
  • Hindu Shahis & Kabul: Kallar founds Hindu Shahi dynasty ruling from Udabhandapura (Hund).
  • Lanka: Sena I repulses Pandya invasion of Anuradhapura.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Lalitaditya Muktapida (Karkota of Kashmir) · 724 CE–760 CE
  • Dharmapala (Pala) · 770 CE–810 CE
  • Amoghavarsha I Nrupatunga (Rashtrakuta) · 814 CE–878 CE
  • Mihira Bhoja I (Gurjara-Pratihara) · 836 CE–885 CE
