Chola Hegemony & Ghaznavid Invasions

चोल नौसैनिक प्रभुत्व और गजनवी
c. 1030 CE · Span: 1000 CE to 1099 CE
Rajaraja I and Rajendra I build an imperial maritime state, subduing Sri Lanka and sending a naval armada to Srivijaya. Mahmud of Ghazni raids northern temples, while the Western Chalukyas rule Kalyani.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Chola Empire: Rajendra I completes conquest of Sri Lanka, marches to the Ganges, and sails across the Bay of Bengal.
  • Western Chalukya Empire (Kalyani): Tailapa II overthrows Rashtrakutas; Someshvara I makes Kalyani the imperial capital.
  • Ghaznavid Empire: Mahmud of Ghazni annexes Punjab to Ghazni, plundering Thanesar, Kannauj, and Somnath.
  • Paramaras of Malwa: Raja Bhoja of Dhar: polymath king, builder of Bhojeshwar temple and Bhojshala university.
  • Pala Empire: Mahipala I and Ramapala revive Buddhist rule across Bengal and Bihar.
  • Chaulukyas (Solankis) of Gujarat: Bhima I rebuilds the stone temple of Somnath following the Ghaznavid raid.
  • Chandelas & Kalachuris: Vidyadhara Chandela repulses Mahmud and builds the Kandariya Mahadeva temple at Khajuraho.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Rajaraja I the Great (Imperial Chola) · 985 CE–1014 CE
  • Rajendra I (Imperial Chola) · 1014 CE–1044 CE
  • Raja Bhoja of Dhar (Paramara) · 1010 CE–1055 CE
