Eve of the Delhi Sultanate

सल्तनत की पूर्वसंध्या
c. 1180 CE · Span: 1100 CE to 1205 CE
Prithviraj Chauhan unites the Rajputs at Ajmer and Delhi. In 1192 CE, Muhammad of Ghor wins the Second Battle of Tarain, opening the plains to Turkish Sultanate conquest. Hoysalas and Kakatiyas rise in the south.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Chahamanas (Chauhans of Ajmer): Prithviraj III rules from Ajmer and Qila Rai Pithora (Delhi); hero of the Tarain battles.
  • Gahadavalas of Kannauj: Jaichandra rules from Varanasi and Kannauj; defeated by Ghorids at Chandawar in 1194.
  • Sena Dynasty of Bengal: Ballala Sena and Lakshmana Sena patronize poet Jayadeva (Gita Govinda); Bakhtiyar Khalji attacks Nabadwip in 1204.
  • Hoysala Empire: Vishnuvardhana and Ballala II build the star-shaped ornate temples of Belur and Halebidu.
  • Kakatiya Dynasty: Prataparudra I and Ganapati Deva make Orugallu (Warangal) their stone-walled capital.
  • Seuna Yadavas of Devagiri: Bhillama V founds Devagiri fortress; flourishing Marathi culture and Hemadpanthi temples.
  • Cholas & Pandyas: Kulottunga III fights the Second Pandya Empire under Maravarman Sundara Pandya.
  • Ghorid Possessions: Muhammad of Ghor's governors establish garrison centers across the northwest.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Prithviraj III Chauhan (Chahamana (Chauhan)) · 1177 CE–1192 CE
