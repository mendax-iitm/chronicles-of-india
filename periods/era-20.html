Early Delhi Sultanate (Mamluks)

प्रारंभिक दिल्ली सल्तनत (मामलूक)
c. 1250 CE · Span: 1206 CE to 1289 CE
Qutb-ud-din Aibak, Iltutmish, Razia Sultana, and Ghiyas-ud-din Balban consolidate Turkish rule across northern India, repulsing early Mongol raids from the Indus.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Delhi Sultanate (Mamluk): Qutb Minar begun; iqta administrative system institutionalized by Iltutmish.
  • Rajput States (Mewar, Ranthambore): Hammiradeva of Ranthambore and Guhilas of Chittor resist Sultanate expansion.
  • Seuna Yadavas of Devagiri: Singhana II expands Yadava power across Maharashtra and northern Karnataka.
  • Kakatiyas of Warangal: Queen Rudrama Devi successfully defends her kingdom, noted by Marco Polo.
  • Hoysala Empire: Narasimha II and Someshvara intervene in Tamil politics from Kannanur Kuppam.
  • Pandya Empire: Sadaiyavarman Sundara Pandya I eliminates the Cholas and becomes master of the South.
  • Eastern Ganga (Odisha): Narasimhadeva I defeats Bengal Muslim forces and builds the Sun Temple at Konark.
  • Ahom Kingdom (Assam): Chaolung Sukaphaa establishes the Ahom kingdom at Charaideo in 1228 CE.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Shams-ud-din Iltutmish (Delhi Sultanate (Mamluk)) · 1211 CE–1236 CE
  • Razia Sultana (Delhi Sultanate (Mamluk)) · 1236 CE–1240 CE
  • Chaolung Sukaphaa (Ahom) · 1228 CE–1268 CE
