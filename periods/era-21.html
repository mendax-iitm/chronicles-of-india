The Khalji Conquests

खिलजी साम्राज्य व दक्षिण अभियान
c. 1315 CE · Span: 1290 CE to 1320 CE
Alauddin Khalji repulses six massive Mongol invasions, annexes Gujarat, Ranthambore, and Chittor, and sends Malik Kafur to conquer the Yadavas, Kakatiyas, Hoysalas, and Pandyas.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Delhi Sultanate (Khalji): Market price control reforms; direct revenue assessment in the doab; Siri fortress.
  • Deccan Tributaries (Devagiri & Warangal): Ramachandra Yadava and Prataparudra II forced to pay massive annual tribute.
  • Hoysala Kingdom: Veera Ballala III defends Karnataka territory following Malik Kafur's sack of Dwarasamudra.
  • Pandya Remnants: Civil war between Sundara and Vira Pandya opens door to Sultanate raid of Madurai.
  • Eastern Ganga (Odisha): Maintaining independence between Bengal and the Sultanate.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Consolidating control over the Upper Brahmaputra valley.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Ala-ud-din Khalji (Delhi Sultanate (Khalji)) · 1296 CE–1316 CE
