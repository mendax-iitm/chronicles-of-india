Tughlaq Empire Zenith

तुगलक साम्राज्य का चरमोत्कर्ष
c. 1335 CE · Span: 1321 CE to 1346 CE
Muhammad bin Tughlaq rules the largest territorial empire in medieval India, annexing Warangal, Madurai, and Bengal. He attempts to transfer the capital to Daulatabad and issue token currency.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Tughlaq Empire: Twenty-three imperial provinces; visited and chronicled by Moroccan traveller Ibn Battuta.
  • Hoysala Kingdom: Veera Ballala III makes Tiruvannamalai a second base before dying in battle against the Madurai Sultanate.
  • Vijayanagara (Sangama Rise): Harihara I and Bukka Raya found Vijayanagara on the Tungabhadra in 1336 CE.
  • Musunuri Nayakas: Provalaya Nayaka and Kapaya Nayaka liberate Warangal from Delhi governor in 1336.
  • Eastern Ganga (Odisha): Bhanudeva II holds the eastern coastline against Delhi incursions.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Rule in Upper Assam unaffected by Delhi authority.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Muhammad bin Tughlaq (Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq)) · 1325 CE–1351 CE
