Bahmani & Vijayanagara Dual Power

बहमनी और विजयनगर युग
c. 1380 CE · Span: 1347 CE to 1397 CE
The Deccan breaks away as Ala-ud-Din Bahman Shah founds the Bahmani Sultanate (1347). South of the Krishna, Vijayanagara under Harihara II unites southern India, while Firuz Shah Tughlaq rebuilds the north.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Vijayanagara Empire: Kumara Kampana conquers the Madurai Sultanate; Hampi becomes a metropolis.
  • Bahmani Sultanate: Capital at Gulbarga (Ahsanabad); fierce wars for the Raichur Doab against Vijayanagara.
  • Delhi Sultanate (Firuz Shah Tughlaq): Extensive canal construction (Western Yamuna canal), hospitals, and new cities (Firozabad, Jaunpur).
  • Bengal Sultanate (Ilyas Shahi): Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah unifies Bengal in 1352; capital at Pandua and Gaur.
  • Gujarat & Malwa (early governors): Sultanate governorships drifting toward autonomy.
  • Eastern Ganga (Odisha): Ganga kings patronizing Odissi culture and Jagannath worship.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Sudangphaa (Bamuni Konwar) introduces Hindu customs to the Ahom court.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Muhammad bin Tughlaq (Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq)) · 1325 CE–1351 CE
