Regional Sultanates & Gajapatis

क्षेत्रीय सल्तनतें और गजपति साम्राज्य
c. 1460 CE · Span: 1398 CE to 1508 CE
Timur’s invasion in 1398 shatters Delhi. Independent sultanates thrive in Gujarat, Malwa, and Jaunpur, while Kapilendra Deva founds the Suryavamsi Gajapati empire in Odisha, rivaling Vijayanagara.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Vijayanagara Empire (Sangama Peak): Deva Raya II enlists Turkish archers, expands trade, and receives Persian envoy Abdur Razzak.
  • Gajapati Empire of Odisha: Kapilendra Deva and Purushottama Deva expand from the Ganges to the Kaveri.
  • Bahmani Sultanate (Bidar phase): Capital moved to Bidar; grand vizier Mahmud Gawan builds the great madrasa before fragmentation.
  • Gujarat Sultanate: Ahmad Shah founds Ahmedabad (1411); Mahmud Begada captures Girnar and Champaner.
  • Malwa Sultanate: Hoshang Shah builds Mandu fortress capital with Jahaz Mahal and marble tombs.
  • Jaunpur (Sharqi Sultanate): 'Shiraz of the East' known for Atala Mosque and Hindustani classical music patronage.
  • Delhi Sultanate (Sayyid & Lodi): Bahlul and Sikandar Lodi restore north Indian authority; Sikandar founds Agra in 1504.
  • Rajput States (Mewar under Kumbha): Rana Kumbha builds 32 forts including Kumbhalgarh and the Vijay Stambha at Chittor.
