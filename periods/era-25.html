Krishnadevaraya’s Golden Age

कृष्णदेवराय का स्वर्ण युग
c. 1520 CE · Span: 1509 CE to 1525 CE
Emperor Krishnadevaraya of Vijayanagara wins the Battle of Raichur, subdues the Gajapatis, and patronizes Telugu and Kannada arts. The Bahmani Sultanate splits into five Deccan Sultanates, while the Portuguese take Goa (1510).

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Vijayanagara Empire: Krishnadevaraya authors Amuktamalyada; Portuguese traveler Domingo Paes describes Hampi as larger than Rome.
  • Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar): Adil Shahi (Bijapur), Nizam Shahi (Ahmadnagar), and Qutb Shahi (Golconda) independent dynasties.
  • Lodi Sultanate (Delhi): Ibrahim Lodi alienates Afghan nobility; Babur invited from Kabul.
  • Gujarat Sultanate: Muzaffar Shah II expands influence into Malwa and fights Portuguese at Diu.
  • Rajput Confederacy (Rana Sanga): Maharana Sanga unites Rajput clans defeating Malwa and Gujarat sultanates.
  • Bengal Sultanate (Husain Shahi): Alauddin Husain Shah patronizes Bengali literature and synthesis.
  • Gajapati Kingdom: Prataparudra Deva makes peace with Krishnadevaraya; Chaitanya Mahaprabhu lives at Puri.

Key Rulers in this Era
