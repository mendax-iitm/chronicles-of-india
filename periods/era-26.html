Early Mughals & the Sur Empire

प्रारंभिक मुग़ल और सूर साम्राज्य
c. 1545 CE · Span: 1526 CE to 1555 CE
Babur wins Panipat (1526) and Khanwa (1527), founding the Mughal realm. In 1540, Sher Shah Suri expels Humayun, establishing the Sur Empire, reforming coinage (the silver Rupiya), and building the Grand Trunk Road.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Sur Empire: Sher Shah Suri introduces administrative sarkars, standardized rupee, and extensive postal dak chaukis.
  • Vijayanagara Empire: Achyuta Deva Raya and Regent Aliya Rama Raya dominate peninsular India.
  • Deccan Sultanates: Bijapur, Golconda, and Ahmadnagar competing with Vijayanagara for border territories.
  • Gujarat Sultanate: Bahadur Shah annexes Malwa but faces Portuguese pressure at Diu.
  • Mughals in Exile (Kabul/Sindh): Humayun flees to Safavid Persia; prepares reconquest of Delhi with Shah Tahmasp's aid.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Suhungmung (Swarganarayan) defeats the Chutiyas and repulses Muslim invasions from Bengal.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Krishnadevaraya (Vijayanagara Empire (Tuluva)) · 1509 CE–1529 CE
  • Zahir-ud-din Babur (Mughal Empire) · 1526 CE–1530 CE
  • Sher Shah Suri (Suri Empire) · 1540 CE–1545 CE
