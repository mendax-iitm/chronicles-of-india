Akbar’s Mughal Empire

अकबर का मुग़ल साम्राज्य
c. 1590 CE · Span: 1556 CE to 1605 CE
Akbar consolidates Mughal power from Kabul and Kashmir to Bengal and the Deccan borders. He institutes the mansabdari system, twelve administrative subahs, and religious tolerance (Sulh-i Kul).

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Mughal Empire: Akbar rules from Agra, Fatehpur Sikri, and Lahore; Raja Todar Mal formulates Dahsala revenue system.
  • Mewar (Maharana Pratap): Maharana Pratap resists Mughal annexation from the Aravalli hills after the Battle of Haldighati (1576).
  • Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar): Chand Bibi defends Ahmadnagar; Ibrahim Adil Shah II writes Kitab-e-Navras at Bijapur.
  • Vijayanagara (Aravidu Dynasty): Following the catastrophe of Talikota (1565), Venkatapati Raya rules from Penukonda and Chandragiri.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Sukhaamphaa (Khora Raja) rules independent Upper Assam.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Jalal-ud-din Akbar the Great (Mughal Empire) · 1556 CE–1605 CE
