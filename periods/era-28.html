Classical Mughal Splendor

मुग़ल स्थापत्य व कला का चरमोत्कर्ष
c. 1640 CE · Span: 1606 CE to 1657 CE
Jahangir and Shah Jahan govern a wealthy, unified empire. Shah Jahan builds the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort, and Shahjahanabad. Mewar signs honorable peace (1615), while Ahmadnagar is annexed.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Mughal Empire: Apex of courtly arts and world GDP share (~24%); Mewar integrated as loyal Rajput ally.
  • Sultanate of Bijapur (Adil Shahi): Muhammad Adil Shah constructs the Gol Gumbaz; expanding into southern Karnataka and Tamil plain.
  • Sultanate of Golconda (Qutb Shahi): Famed Kollur diamond mines (producing the Koh-i-Noor); flourishing Indo-Persian culture.
  • Nayakas of the South: Tirumala Nayaka of Madurai and Nayakas of Thanjavur and Ikkeri assert sovereign rule.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Pratap Singha introduces the Paik labor-mobilization system.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Shah Jahan (Mughal Empire) · 1628 CE–1658 CE
