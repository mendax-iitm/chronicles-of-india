Aurangzeb & Shivaji’s Swarajya

औरंगज़ेब और छत्रपति शिवाजी
c. 1685 CE · Span: 1658 CE to 1706 CE
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj carves out Maratha Swarajya using hill forts and ganimi kava (guerrilla tactics). Aurangzeb annexes Bijapur and Golconda, engaging in a grueling 25-year war in the Deccan.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Mughal Empire: Territorially largest extent (annexing Bijapur 1686, Golconda 1687), but severe fiscal overextension.
  • Maratha Swarajya: Shivaji crowned at Raigad (1674); southern campaign captures Gingee and Vellore; Santaji and Dhanaji wage war of liberation.
  • Kingdom of Mysore (Wodeyars): Chikka Devaraja Wodeyar centralizes the administration and army of Mysore.
  • Nayakas & Poligars of Madurai: Queen Mangammal reigns as regent in Madurai, building extensive road networks.
  • Ahom Kingdom: Lachit Borphukan defeats Ram Singh's Mughal army at the Battle of Saraighat (1671).

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Shah Jahan (Mughal Empire) · 1628 CE–1658 CE
  • Aurangzeb Alamgir (Mughal Empire) · 1658 CE–1707 CE
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (Maratha Swarajya) · 1674 CE–1680 CE
