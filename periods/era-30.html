Maratha Zenith & Fragmentation

मराठा साम्राज्य का उत्कर्ष
c. 1750 CE · Span: 1707 CE to 1760 CE
Following Aurangzeb's death, Peshwa Baji Rao I expands the Maratha Confederacy across Malwa, Gujarat, and Bundelkhand, reaching Delhi. Autonomous successor states arise in Hyderabad, Bengal, and Awadh.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Maratha Confederacy: Chhatrapati Shahu and Peshwas (Balaji Vishwanath, Baji Rao I, Balaji Baji Rao); Scindia, Holkar, Gaekwad, and Bhonsle branches.
  • Nizam of Hyderabad (Asaf Jahi): Nizam-ul-Mulk Asaf Jah I establishes hereditary autonomy in the Deccan in 1724.
  • Nawabs of Bengal: Murshid Quli Khan, Alivardi Khan, and Siraj-ud-Daulah rule from Murshidabad.
  • Nawabs of Awadh: Saadat Ali Khan I and Safdarjung govern rich Gangetic plains from Lucknow and Faizabad.
  • Mughal Remnant (Delhi): Emperor Muhammad Shah sacked by Nader Shah of Persia in 1739; titular imperial prestige remains.
  • Durrani Empire (Afghan): Ahmad Shah Durrani establishes Afghan empire, contesting Punjab with the Marathas and Sikhs.
  • Kingdom of Travancore: Marthanda Varma crushes the Dutch East India Company at the Battle of Colachel (1741).

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Aurangzeb Alamgir (Mughal Empire) · 1658 CE–1707 CE
  • Peshwa Baji Rao I (Maratha Confederacy) · 1720 CE–1740 CE
  • Anizham Thirunal Marthanda Varma (Kingdom of Travancore) · 1729 CE–1758 CE
  • Mir Qamar-ud-din Khan (Asaf Jah I) (State of Hyderabad (Asaf Jahi)) · 1724 CE–1748 CE
