Marathas, Mysore & Company Rise

मराठा पुनरुत्थान, मैसूर और कंपनी
c. 1785 CE · Span: 1761 CE to 1798 CE
Despite heavy losses at the Third Battle of Panipat (1761), Mahadji Shinde rebuilds Maratha dominance in Delhi. The British East India Company wins Plassey (1757) and Buxar (1764), while Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan fight four wars in Mysore.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Maratha Confederacy: Mahadji Scindia and Nana Fadnavis defeat the British in the First Anglo-Maratha War (Treaty of Salbai, 1782).
  • British East India Company: Clive and Warren Hastings govern Bengal diwani, Northern Circars, and Carnatic protectorates.
  • Kingdom of Mysore: Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan employ modern rocket artillery, resisting British expansion in Carnatic.
  • Nizam of Hyderabad: Nizam Ali Khan navigates shifting alliances between Marathas, Mysore, and the British.
  • Nawabs of Awadh: Asaf-ud-Daula builds the Bara Imambara in Lucknow; subsidiary alliance signed.
  • Sikh Misls (Punjab): Twelve sovereign Sikh confederacies (Dal Khalsa) liberate Punjab from Afghan control.
  • Durrani Afghanistan: Zaman Shah Durrani ruling western marches.
  • Travancore: Dharma Raja maintains alliance with British against Mysore.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Peshwa Madhavrao I (Maratha Confederacy) · 1761 CE–1772 CE
  • Tipu Sultan (Tiger of Mysore) (Kingdom of Mysore) · 1782 CE–1799 CE
  • Warren Hastings (British East India Company) · 1773 CE–1785 CE
