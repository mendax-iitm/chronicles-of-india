Company Hegemony & the Sikh Empire

कंपनी का प्रभुत्व और सिख साम्राज्य
c. 1835 CE · Span: 1818 CE to 1848 CE
The Third Anglo-Maratha War (1818) extinguishes the Peshwaship, leaving the Company master of India. In the northwest, Maharaja Ranjit Singh builds the formidable Sikh Khalsa Sarkar from Kashmir to Peshawar.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • British East India Company (Direct): Bengal, Madras, and Bombay Presidencies dominate subcontinent governance.
  • Sikh Empire (Sarkar-i Khalsa): Ranjit Singh's modernized Fauj-i-Khas commands European generals; conquers Multan, Kashmir, and Peshawar.
  • Princely States (Subsidiary Alliance): Over 500 autonomous states bound by British paramountcy: Hyderabad, Mysore, Gwalior, Rajputana.
  • Sindh (Talpur): Annexed by Sir Charles Napier in 1843 ('Peccavi').
  • Kabul / Durrani Remnants: Dost Mohammad Khan restores Barakzai rule following First Anglo-Afghan War.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Maharaja Ranjit Singh (Sher-e-Punjab) (Sikh Empire) · 1799 CE–1839 CE
  • Lord Dalhousie (British East India Company) · 1848 CE–1856 CE
