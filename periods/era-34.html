Doctrine of Lapse & the 1857 Revolt

डलहौजी की हड़प नीति और 1857 की क्रांति
c. 1856 CE · Span: 1849 CE to 1857 CE
Lord Dalhousie annexes Punjab (1849), Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur, and Awadh (1856) through the Doctrine of Lapse. In May 1857, sepoys at Meerut revolt, sparking a widespread war of independence.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • British East India Company: Annexations of Punjab (1849) and Awadh (1856); first railways (1853) and telegraph lines.
  • Princely States: Jammu and Kashmir sold to Gulab Singh (1846); major states remain neutral or aid British in 1857.
  • 1857 Uprising Centers (Delhi, Awadh, Jhansi): Bahadur Shah Zafar proclaimed Emperor; Rani Lakshmibai, Nana Saheb, Tantia Tope, and Kunwar Singh lead fierce resistance.

Key Rulers in this Era
