c. 1910 CE · Span: 1858 CE to 1946 CE
Queen Victoria's 1858 Proclamation dissolves the Company; direct Crown rule governs British India alongside 565 Princely States under paramountcy. The Indian national freedom movement arises under Congress, Tilak, and Gandhi.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • British India (Direct Provinces): Governed by the Viceroy and Provincial Governors: Bengal, Bombay, Madras, United Provinces, Punjab, Central Provinces, Assam, Bihar-Orissa.
  • Princely States (Indian Rulers): 565 states with internal autonomy under British paramountcy: Kashmir, Hyderabad, Mysore, Baroda, Gwalior, Travancore, Rajputana states.

Key Rulers in this Era

  • Queen Victoria (Empress of India) (British Crown (Raj)) · 1858 CE–1901 CE
