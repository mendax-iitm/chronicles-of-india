Independence & Partition

स्वतंत्रता और विभाजन
August 1947 · Span: 1947 CE to 1947 CE
On 14–15 August 1947, British rule ends with the partition of British India into India and Pakistan. Sardar Patel leads the rapid integration of 565 princely states into the Indian Union.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Dominion of India: Independent India under Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru; Sardar Patel integrates princely territories.
  • Dominion of Pakistan: West and East Pakistan (divided by the Radcliffe Line) under Governor-General Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
  • Acceding / Unintegrated States (Aug 1947): Jammu & Kashmir signs Instrument of Accession in Oct 1947; Hyderabad integrated via Police Action in Sep 1948.
