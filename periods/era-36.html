Independence of India & National Integration

भारत की स्वतंत्रता एवं राष्ट्रीय एकीकरण
15 August – October 1947 · Span: 1947 CE to 1947 CE
On 15 August 1947, India achieves independence from British colonial rule under Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru. Deputy Prime Minister Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and V.P. Menon rapidly integrate 565 princely states into the sovereign Union of India (Bharat). On 26 October 1947, Maharaja Hari Singh executes the Instrument of Accession, integrating Jammu & Kashmir into the Indian Union. Pakistan is carved out across West Punjab, Sindh, NWFP, Balochistan, and East Bengal.

Territorial Polities & Realms

  • Union of India (Bharat): Sovereign Union of India under Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru and Home Minister Sardar Patel. Includes British Indian provinces and acceding princely states. Jammu & Kashmir formally acceded on 26 October 1947 via the Instrument of Accession.
  • Hyderabad State (Standstill Agreement, 1947): Ruled by Nizam Mir Osman Ali Khan; signed a one-year Standstill Agreement with the Government of India on 29 November 1947. Integrated into the Indian Union following Sardar Patel's Police Action (Operation Polo) in September 1948.
  • Dominion of Pakistan: West and East Pakistan (demarcated by the Radcliffe Line Award) under Governor-General Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
  • Kingdom of Afghanistan: Independent Kingdom of Afghanistan ruled by King Mohammed Zahir Shah (r. 1933–1973).
  • Dominion of Ceylon: British Crown colony achieving self-governing dominion status in February 1948 under Prime Minister D.S. Senanayake.
